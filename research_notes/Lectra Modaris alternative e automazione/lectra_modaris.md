## 1. What is Lectra, what is Modaris, and which editions/related products exist today?

### Takeaway
Lectra is a French industrial-software and cutting-equipment group, founded in Bordeaux in 1973 and listed on Euronext Paris. It makes about half its revenue in fashion. Since it bought Gerber Technology in June 2021, it also owns Gerber AccuMark, the other big pattern CAD, so it controls the two most widely used professional pattern-making systems. Modaris is Lectra's pattern-making CAD. In 2026 it is sold in tiers (Essential / Classic / Expert, with 3D prototyping capabilities) and uses year-based versions released twice a year (latest: **V2026.1**, spring 2026). It sits inside a wider Lectra suite: Kaledo (design), Diamino (marker making), Valia Fashion (cloud order-to-cut), Fashion On Demand, Vector cutters and Alys plotters, Gerber AccuMark, and from September 2026 Apogy (agentic-AI product development).

## 2. What exactly does Modaris do, and which functions sit in which tier?

### Takeaway
Modaris covers the whole technical "paper pattern" stage in software: drawing or importing patterns (including digitizing paper patterns on a digitizing table), modifying them, grading them into all sizes, finishing them for production (seam allowances, notches, grain-line axes, metadata), and in 3D-enabled versions sewing them virtually onto an avatar with simulated fabrics. Finished patterns then go to marker making (Diamino), plotting and cutting. The basic tiers differ mainly in whether you can **create** patterns (Classic and up), only grade and finish existing ones (Essential), or use **parametric** automation and advanced 3D (Expert).

### Cited Findings
- Core functions: "create, modify, grade and industrialize patterns"; graded base-pattern libraries (Classic) — [Lectra Modaris](https://www.lectra.com/en/fashion/products/modaris)
- Finishing for production: pieces are finalized "by adding notches and seam values, defining axes and giving all the information necessary to ensure a smooth production process" — [Lectra Modaris](https://www.lectra.com/en/fashion/products/modaris) (snippet); see also [Lectra: Resolve technical design issues as of the pattern development stage](https://www.lectra.com/en/library/solving-the-challenges-of-pattern-development-with-advanced-cad-software)
- Seam and notch control: Modaris Classic can match two pieces together using a key measurement with developed points. Mismatched notches cause seam-quality problems — [Lectra library snippet](https://www.lectra.com/en/library/solving-the-challenges-of-pattern-development-with-advanced-cad-software)
- Grading: newer tools handle "very extended size systems", including pieces whose shape changes by size, and improved "oriented grading" for curves graded in parallel (V8-era feature notes) — [CongNgheMay: What's new in Modaris V8](http://www.congnghemay.info/2017/03/whats-new-version-lectra-modaris-v8/) (secondary source, 2017). Size grading includes both automatic and manual methods — [O'Reilly: *Pattern Cutting for Clothing Using CAD*, ch. 11 "Creating size ranges in Lectra Modaris"](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/pattern-cutting-for/9780857092311/xhtml/B978085709231150011X.htm)
- Digitizing paper patterns: Modaris needs specific digitizing programs plus an **interactive digitizing table** (a large tablet on which an operator traces a paper pattern point by point with a cursor) — [ResearchGate: Introducing Digitizing Technology in CAD Pattern-Making Class](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/348671183_Introducing_Digitizing_Technology_in_CAD_Pattern-Making_Class_for_Upcycling_Project)
- 3D virtual prototyping: build 3D prototypes "with vast libraries of materials and colors using your patterns and configurable or imported mannequins". Patterns can be adjusted during virtual prototyping. A fabric library is "categorized according to their characteristics". Since V8R5 (2023), multiple garments can be simulated on one avatar — [Lectra Modaris](https://www.lectra.com/en/fashion/products/modaris); [Lectra: Modaris V8R5](https://www.lectra.com/en/library/modaris-v8r5-is-now-available)
- 2D↔3D sync and placing prints and logos in 3D (Expert 3D) — [Lectra Modaris](https://www.lectra.com/en/fashion/products/modaris)
- Parametric editing (Expert): "save up to 30% of pattern adjustment time" (vendor claim) — [Lectra Modaris Expert](https://www.lectra.com/en/products/modaris-expert)
- Real-world example: Falconeri (knitwear, Calzedonia group) reports 25% faster product development and patterns produced 10% faster with Modaris Expert (vendor case study) — [Lectra: Falconeri case study](https://www.lectra.com/en/library/falconeri-streamlines-product-development-processes-with-modaris-expert-patternmaking)
- Hand-off downstream: Diamino "integrates with Lectra Modaris to ensure pattern consistency" and builds the markers used for cutting — [Lectra Fashion On Demand / Diamino snippets](https://www.lectra.com/en/products/fashion-on-demand-by-lectra). Italian CAD/CAM bureaus describe the typical chain: automatic markers from DXF files, HPGL files sent to plotters, ISO files sent to cutters — [Devid srl](https://www.devidsrl.it/taglio-tessuti-cam-cad/)
- Training references: the textbook *Digital Pattern Cutting for Fashion with Lectra Modaris: From 2D Pattern Modification to 3D Prototyping* (Bloomsbury, ISBN 9781350065123), by an Arts University Bournemouth lecturer — [AUB news](https://aub.ac.uk/latest/fashion-lecturer-publishes-book-lectra-modaris-2). Also *Modaris & Diamino for Apparel Design* by Catherine Black — [eBay listing](https://www.ebay.de/itm/336399050282)

### Inferences
- A university that licenses only Classic (or an older version, or no 3D) gives students full 2D drafting and grading but not parametric automation or virtual fitting. A school with no Diamino licence cannot teach marker making in the same environment. This is a likely source of the niece's "the university doesn't have all functions".
- Modaris is a technical, production-oriented tool for patternmakers. It is not a sketching or illustration tool; that role belongs to Kaledo or Adobe tools.

- The detailed V2026.1 feature list could not be read. Only the webinar summary was available.

## 3. Licensing and pricing: subscription vs perpetual, typical costs, dongles, and why it is "expensive"

### Takeaway
Lectra does **not** publish prices. Modaris (and AccuMark) are now sold mainly as **annual subscriptions** with 1–3 year commitments. The fee covers the software, remote technical support and the two upgrades a year. Older customers on **perpetual licences** (protected by USB dongles / FlexLM licence files) are pushed to trade in for a discounted subscription. Public price figures exist only from aggregators and old forums, and they vary a lot. One aggregator figure says **"Modaris Classic from US$2,770/year"**. Industry commentary describes enterprise deals historically in the **five figures per year**. Expert, 3D, Diamino and hardware (plotter, digitizer, cutter) all add cost.

### Cited Findings
**Official (verified from Lectra materials)**
- Subscription: "by paying an annual fee, you can benefit from an all-inclusive service package that includes the CAD software, Lectra's remote technical support, and regular upgrades". Commitment periods run from 1 to 3 years. Lectra says there are no "additional, hidden or unexpected costs" during the period — [Lectra factsheet: Modaris and Gerber AccuMark subscription plan (2023)](https://www.lectra.com/sites/default/files/2023-03/factsheet-subscription-modaris-gerber-accumark-en.pdf); [Lectra: Why you should switch to a CAD software subscription](https://www.lectra.com/en/library/why-you-should-switch-to-modaris-and-gerber-accumark-software-subscriptions)
- Perpetual-to-subscription: customers can "trade in your current license to subscribe at a discounted rate for the first two years", or keep the perpetual licence and buy additional subscriptions — [Lectra subscription page](https://www.lectra.com/en/library/why-you-should-switch-to-modaris-and-gerber-accumark-software-subscriptions); [Lectra: Convert AccuMark perpetual license](https://www.lectra.com/en/library/how-to-convert-your-gerber-accumark-perpetual-license-to-a-new-subscription)
- Business-model context: 75% of Lectra's 2025 revenue was recurring and SaaS grew 14%, so the group is deliberately moving customers to subscriptions — [GlobeNewswire 2025 results](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/02/11/3236607/0/en/LECTRA-2025-results-growth-in-recurring-revenues-and-operational-resilience.html)
- Upgrades for subscribers or service-contract holders come via MyLectra — [Lectra: latest version of Modaris](https://www.lectra.com/en/library/what-to-know-about-the-latest-version-of-modaris)

**Licence protection (third-party technical sources)**
- Lectra products use **FLEXid USB dongles** (each with a unique ID) and/or a **FLEXlm licence server** with `.lic` files over a network — [Autogarment: Installing FLEXid for Lectra FlexLM license server](https://autogarment.com/installing-flexid-number-for-lectra-flexlm-license-server/) (third-party blog)

Modaris runs on Windows.

### Inferences
- "Very expensive" is accurate by design. Modaris is priced as industrial B2B software. It is sold per seat and per module, usually quote-based, and the subscription bundles support and upgrades. A company equips a patternmaking room with several seats and usually adds Diamino, a plotter and sometimes a cutter, so totals reach thousands to tens of thousands of euros per year. For an individual, even the reported entry figure (~$2.8k/year) is high compared with consumer software.
- The move from perpetual licences to subscriptions means a freelancer can no longer "buy once and keep". Stopping payment usually stops updates and, under subscription, use.
- The many cracked "Modaris + dongle emulator" pages in the search results show strong demand from people who cannot afford licences. They are illegal and often carry malware. Steer readers to academic licences instead.

- No official or reseller price list, Italian quote or 2026 price in euros was found. The real cost per seat for Expert, 3D or Diamino is unknown.
- I could not confirm whether new perpetual licences of Modaris can still be bought in 2026.

## 4. Education programmes, student licences, Italian schools, and why a university may not have all functions

### Takeaway
Lectra runs a large education programme. It says it works with 800+ universities and that more than 2,000 schools in 100+ countries use its technology, and it has given 100,000+ free licences to students (counts differ between Lectra pages). Schools sign partnership agreements, a small "Privilege Partner" tier gets closer ties, and students at participating schools can get **free time-limited personal licences**, typically 12 months. In Italy, Politecnico di Milano (Kaledo, Modaris, Diamino), LABA Rimini (50 free one-year licences), Scuola di Moda Vitali (Lectra Education Certified; Modaris Expert + Diamino on students' laptops for 12–24 months) and many private technical schools teach Modaris. What a university can offer depends on which modules, versions and seat counts its agreement covers, so missing functions (3D, Expert parametrics, Diamino, plotters) are common.

### Cited Findings
- Lectra says it "shares its solutions and expertise with over 800 universities around the world", has provided "over 100,000 software licenses free of charge to students", and that "more than 2,000 schools" across "over 100 countries" use its technology — [Lectra: Lectra supports the new generation of fashion professionals](https://www.lectra.com/en/library/lectra-supports-the-new-generation-of-fashion-professionals) (snippet). ## 5. File formats and interoperability

### Takeaway
Modaris saves in proprietary Lectra formats: modern **.MDL** (model) files, and older paired **.IBA** (pieces) + **.VET** (model) files. To exchange patterns with other CAD systems (Gerber, Optitex, CLO 3D, Tukatech, Audaces), it exports and imports the industry's **DXF-AAMA** and **DXF-ASTM** pattern formats. These are DXF drawings with layer conventions plus grade-rule (.RUL) data. Since the Lectra–Gerber merger, Gerber AccuMark can open Modaris V8 files directly.

### Cited Findings
- .MDL is the current proprietary Modaris format, holding 2D/3D pattern and model information. The older format was a pair of .IBA (pieces) and .VET (model) files that must stay together — [FileFormat.com: IBA file format](https://docs.fileformat.com/cad/iba/); [CLO community: ".aama or .mdl file required by manufacturer"](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/360024064753--aama-or-mdl-file-required-by-manufacturer)
- Modaris exports DXF-AAMA and DXF-ASTM. Tutorials exist for V8+ and for V7/V6 — [YouTube: Exportar AAMA o ASTM DXF desde Lectra Modaris V8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aeQ3OxyE3vc); [YouTube: V7/V6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k0eGtuC36JQ); [McNeel (Rhino) forum: Export file for Modaris; need DXF AAMA or ASTM](https://discourse.mcneel.com/t/export-file-for-modaris-lectra-need-dxf-aama-or-astm/9708)
- "AAMA is really just DXF with a few layer conventions" — [McNeel forum](https://discourse.mcneel.com/t/export-file-for-modaris-lectra-need-dxf-aama-or-astm/9708/2). ASTM/AAMA DXF can carry graded nests and grade-rule tables, unlike a plain DXF — [Gerber community: Standard DXF vs ASTM/AAMA DXF](https://gerbertechnology.vanillacommunity.com/discussion/1305/what-is-the-difference-between-a-standard-dxf-file-and-an-astm-aama-dxf-file)
- **ASTM D6673** ("Standard Practice for Sewn Products Pattern Data Interchange—Data Format"): exchanges 2D pattern pieces as DXF (based on AutoCAD DXF R13) and grade-rule tables as a formatted ASCII file. **ASTM D6673-10 was withdrawn in 2019**, but the format is still widely used in practice — [iTeh Standards: ASTM D6673-10 (Withdrawn 2019)](https://standards.iteh.ai/catalog/standards/astm/3ea6d7eb-457d-4601-8b2a-568fe262a910/astm-d6673-10); [Patro docs: DXF ASTM format](https://fabricesalvaire.github.io/Patro/resources/file-format/dxf-astm.html)
- AAMA is the older, more common variant and uses a separate .RUL grade-rule file. ASTM puts more data inside the file — [i-digit: DXF import & export problems](https://www.i-digit.co.uk/post/dxf-import-export-problems) (consultancy blog)
- CLO 3D imports and exports 2D patterns as DXF-AAMA/ASTM, which is the usual Modaris→CLO route — [CLO support: 2D Pattern (DXF) Import/Export](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000493067-2D-Pattern-DXF-Import-Export)
- **Modaris ↔ Gerber AccuMark:** after the merger, AccuMark users can convert Modaris patterns with the Data Conversion Utility or Pattern Design and **open Modaris V8 files directly**. This avoids converting MDL to DXF-AAMA/ASTM first and the data loss that can bring (announced Dec 2021) — [Lectra: Gerber AccuMark and Modaris are now compatible](https://www.lectra.com/en/library/gerber-accumark-and-modaris-are-now-compatible); [Just Style](https://www.just-style.com/news/lectra-gerber-boost-productivity-with-patternmaking-solution-interoperability/); [Specialty Fabrics Review, 10 Dec 2021](https://specialtyfabricsreview.com/2021/12/10/lectra-and-gerber-announce-patternmaking-solution/)
- Output to machines: HPGL files for plotters and ISO files for cutters are standard in the CAD/CAM chain — [Devid srl](https://www.devidsrl.it/taglio-tessuti-cam-cad/). Paid conversion bureaus translate between Lectra, Gerber, Investronica, Optitex and Tukatech — [Apparel BPO](https://www.apparel-bpo.com/apparel-file-%20pattern-conversion-dxf-lectra-gerber-investronica%20-optitex-tukatech-hpgl.html)

### Inferences
- A student can keep personal copies of their work as DXF-AAMA/ASTM. These files open in other, cheaper CADs and in CLO 3D, which protects the work if the Modaris licence expires. Native .MDL files open only in Lectra (and in recent AccuMark).
- DXF conversion is "lossy" in practice: some construction history, parametric links and 3D data are not carried over. That is why Lectra built direct MDL reading into AccuMark.

### Gaps
- No official Lectra list of every Modaris import/export format for 2026 was retrieved. A search summary listed formats such as "Lectra MOD/CIT/LAY" and "Gerber ZIP", but without a reliable source, so they are left out.

## 7. Basic fashion-industry concepts needed to understand Modaris (plain-language glossary for a non-expert)

### Takeaway
Modaris digitizes one stage of how clothing is made industrially. The stage is: design sketch → **pattern** (*cartamodello*, the flat templates of each garment piece, made by the *modellista* through *modellistica*) → **grading** (*sviluppo taglie*, scaling the pattern to every size) → **industrialization** (adding seam allowances, notches, grain lines) → **marker making** (*piazzamento*, arranging all pieces of all sizes on the fabric width to waste as little fabric as possible) → **plotting** (printing the marker or patterns on paper with a large-format plotter) → **cutting** (*taglio*, by hand or with an automatic cutter such as Lectra Vector). Files move between systems as DXF-AAMA/ASTM.

### Cited Findings
- **Cartamodello / pattern & modellistica:** a pattern is built from lines, curves, seam allowances, notches and holes. Pieces are "finalized" with notches, seam values and axes before production — [Lectra Modaris](https://www.lectra.com/en/fashion/products/modaris); [Lectra library](https://www.lectra.com/en/library/solving-the-challenges-of-pattern-development-with-advanced-cad-software)
- **Sviluppo taglie / grading:** to get garments in every size that are proportionally and visually the same as the prototype, the *sviluppo taglia* spreads the millimetres that separate one size from the next, using criteria similar to those used to build the base patterns — [Creasolution: Sviluppo taglie abbigliamento](https://www.creasolution.it/news-eventi/evento/sviluppo-taglie-per-abbigliamento-tutti-i-segreti-per-un-risultato-perfetto/). Grade rules and size tables are exchanged as grade-rule tables in AAMA/ASTM — [ASTM D6673](https://standards.iteh.ai/catalog/standards/astm/3ea6d7eb-457d-4601-8b2a-568fe262a910/astm-d6673-10)
- **Piazzamento / marker making:** the computer-generated arrangement of pattern pieces on the fabric, used to minimize fabric consumption and waste and to estimate fabric cost. In Lectra's suite this is Diamino (or Valia Fashion in the cloud) — [Lectra Diamino PDF](https://www.lectra.com/sites/default/files/lectra/press_detail/diaminofashion_v6r2_en.pdf); [Devid srl](https://www.devidsrl.it/taglio-tessuti-cam-cad/)
- **Plotter:** large-format machines that draw markers or patterns on paper (HPGL files). Cutting plotters physically cut paper patterns or light materials from the CAD vector file; "the cutting plotter doesn't print, it cuts" — [Caledonia Group: plotter da taglio nel tessile-abbigliamento](https://www.caledoniagroup.it/plotter-da-taglio-professionale-nel-settore-tessile-abbigliamento/); [Devid srl](https://www.devidsrl.it/taglio-tessuti-cam-cad/)
- **Taglio / cutting:** industrial cutting rooms use automatic cutters (e.g. Lectra Vector) fed by the marker. Lectra sells integrated cutting-room solutions that combine marker making, pattern making, spreading and cutting — [Lectra Vector](https://www.lectra.com/en/furniture/products/vector-furniture); [Lectra Fashion On Demand](https://www.lectra.com/en/products/fashion-on-demand-by-lectra)
- **Digitizing:** turning a physical paper pattern into a digital one, using a digitizing table or scanning plus cleanup — [ResearchGate paper](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/348671183_Introducing_Digitizing_Technology_in_CAD_Pattern-Making_Class_for_Upcycling_Project)
- **DXF-AAMA / DXF-ASTM:** standard "neutral" pattern files (DXF drawings with apparel-specific layers plus grading data) that let different pattern CADs exchange patterns — [Gerber community](https://gerbertechnology.vanillacommunity.com/discussion/1305/what-is-the-difference-between-a-standard-dxf-file-and-an-astm-aama-dxf-file); [ASTM D6673](https://standards.iteh.ai/catalog/standards/astm/3ea6d7eb-457d-4601-8b2a-568fe262a910/astm-d6673-10)
- **3D virtual prototyping:** sewing the 2D pattern pieces virtually around a digital mannequin (avatar) with simulated fabric properties, to check fit before making a physical sample — [Lectra Modaris](https://www.lectra.com/en/fashion/products/modaris); [University of Manchester (Modaris 3D Fit)](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/about/news/university-adopts-lectras-3d-cad-for-textiles-and-clothing/)

### Inferences
- A simple analogy for the Italian non-expert: Modaris is to the *modellista* what AutoCAD is to an architect. It does not design the dress's look. It produces the exact technical "templates" for all sizes and sends them to the cutting room.
