# Commercial (paid) alternatives to Lectra Modaris for pattern-making CAD and 3D garment prototyping (state as of Oct 2026)

> **How these notes were made (read first):** In this session the fetch tool was blocked for every vendor domain tried (clo3d.com, support.clo3d.com, marvelousdesigner.com, style3d.com, browzwear.com, tukaweb.com, lectra.com, designarcacademy.com). So every finding below comes from **search-engine summaries of the linked pages**, not from reading the pages directly. Prices should be spot-checked on the vendor page before anyone buys. Reliability labels used below: **[OFFICIAL]** = vendor's own site/help centre/press release; **[SCHOOL]** = school's own page; **[3RD-PARTY]** = review/aggregator/SEO site (lower reliability); **[ANECDOTAL]** = forum or user report.
> Currency: vendors publish in USD (BRL for Audaces Brazil). Approximate EUR conversions use **1 USD ≈ €0.85–0.90** and **1 EUR ≈ R$6.2–6.4**. These rates are assumptions and were not checked for Oct 2026.

## Q1. For each tool: what it does, overlap with Modaris (2D drafting, grading, marker making, 3D), price, platform, learning resources, adoption (esp. Italy/EU)

### Takeaway
Only a few tools match Modaris across the board (2D drafting + grading + marker/nesting): Gerber AccuMark (now also Lectra), Optitex, Audaces, Tukatech, Grafis, cad.assyst, PAD, Richpeace/Boke. None of these has a cheap self-serve student licence in Europe, except **TUKAcad Learning Edition (~$19/month)** and **PAD Basic (~$15/month)**. The 3D tools (CLO, Browzwear, Style3D, Marvelous Designer) are much cheaper for students. CLO is the strongest of them for pattern work, but it does **not** replace Modaris for production grading and markers.

### Cited Findings

#### Lectra Modaris (baseline, for comparison)
- Italian schools in the Lectra Education Program give students Modaris for home use. LABA Rimini offers **50 free Modaris licences for one year** to Fashion Design 2 and 3 students (A.Y. 2025-26). Students must have their own Windows laptop and be on track with their studies (not *fuoricorso*). The licence renews automatically each year on enrolment. Requests close 1 December. — [LABA Rimini, licenza Modaris [SCHOOL]](https://www.labarimini.com/licenza-modaris-modellistica-cad-per-studenti/)
- Modaris runs **only on Windows**. LABA's stated requirements: Windows 10/11 **Pro/Enterprise** 64-bit, Intel i3/i5/i7 at 2.2 GHz or faster, 8 GB+ RAM, 200 GB SSD. — [LABA Rimini [SCHOOL]](https://www.labarimini.com/licenza-modaris-modellistica-cad-per-studenti/)
- Scuola di Moda Vitali (a Lectra Certified partner) lets every enrolled student install **Modaris V8R2 Expert + Diamino** (marker making) on their own PC for 12 months. Its paid CAD course packages cost €2,200–2,500 including VAT and come with the licence. — [Scuola Vitali, Promozione CAD Lectra [SCHOOL]](https://www.scuolavitali.com/promozione-cad-lectra/)
- Polimoda and NABA are reported as members of the Lectra Education Program, which "provides licenses for student laptops". Istituto di Moda Treviso is a Certified Education Partner. — [innovationintextiles.com, "Teaching tomorrow's fashion talents with Lectra"](https://www.innovationintextiles.com/teaching-tomorrows-fashion-talents-with-lectra/) (taken from the search summary of the Lectra-education results)
- Lectra sells Modaris and Gerber AccuMark as annual subscriptions of 1 or 3 years, with remote support and upgrades included. The price is not published. — [Lectra subscription factsheet (2023) [OFFICIAL]](https://www.lectra.com/sites/default/files/2023-03/factsheet-subscription-modaris-gerber-accumark-en.pdf); [Lectra blog [OFFICIAL]](https://www.lectra.com/en/library/why-you-should-switch-to-modaris-and-gerber-accumark-software-subscriptions)

### Inferences
- **Comparable to Modaris (2D + grading + marker):** AccuMark, Optitex, Audaces (Moldes + Encaixe), TUKAcad, Grafis (CAD + marker), cad.assyst, Richpeace/Boke, PAD.
- **3D-first tools** with some 2D pattern editing and only limited grading/marker for production: CLO, Browzwear, Style3D Studio.
- **Pure CG cloth tools**, not production pattern CAD: Marvelous Designer and Style3D Atelier.
- **Home-sewing tools** (Garment Designer, PatternMaker, Wild Ginger, Sewist): limited AAMA support or none confirmed. They are hobby-oriented and not suitable for a university fashion curriculum built on Modaris.
- **Italy:** Lectra appears dominant in fashion education, given the many Lectra-certified schools found (Vitali, LABA, Burgo, Treviso, Polimoda, NABA, CFP Lonati, Formodena). CLO is the main 3D tool taught (IED, Marangoni). Audaces has an Italian web presence, but no Italian school partnership or Italian student price was found.
- **Platforms (background knowledge, not checked this session):** CLO, Marvelous Designer and Browzwear VStitcher run on Windows and macOS (Lotta is historically Mac-oriented). Gerber AccuMark, Optitex, TUKAcad, Audaces, Grafis, Richpeace and Modaris are Windows-centric. Modaris being Windows-only is confirmed by LABA. This matters if the niece has a Mac.
- **Ease of learning (inference from training ecosystem):** CLO has the largest set of tutorials and Italian school courses. Modaris/AccuMark have strong school training in Italy. Audaces' learning material is mainly in Portuguese. TUKAcad has US-centric material (University of Fashion).

## Q3. Which tools import/export DXF-AAMA/ASTM, so patterns made at home open in Modaris at university and vice versa?

### Takeaway
DXF-AAMA (and ASTM) is the common exchange format: Modaris imports and exports it, as do CLO, Browzwear, Style3D Studio, TUKAcad, Audaces, Grafis, AccuMark and Seamly2D. No third-party tool writes native Modaris .MDL. So moving files between home and school always means AAMA/ASTM DXF plus checking scale, notches and grade rules after import. Expect some cleanup.

### Cited Findings
- **CLO:** exports DXF-AAMA, DXF-ASTM and standard DXF. Grading can go inside the DXF or as a separate .RUL rule file. AAMA has an option to drop non-graded points. CLO cannot export Lectra .MDL ("export DXF-AAMA with RUL files for grading... but not the MDL format"). Gerber/Lectra ZIP archives cannot be opened in CLO. — [CLO support, 2D Pattern (DXF) Import/Export [OFFICIAL]](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000493067-2D-Pattern-DXF-Import-Export); [CLO forum, ".aama or .mdl file required by manufacturer" [ANECDOTAL]](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/360024064753--aama-or-mdl-file-required-by-manufacturer); [CLO forum, Best Gerber/Lectra file type [ANECDOTAL]](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/30524536191769-Best-Gerber-Lectra-file-type-for-use-in-ClO3D)
- **Known CLO→Modaris issue:** patterns from CLO AAMA DXF imported into Modaris "10× smaller" (100 cm became 10 cm). The fix is matching units/scale in Modaris import settings; Lectra can make a custom import setup, and some export from CLO at 1000% as a workaround. — [CLO forum, "Opening a CLO made AAMA DXF file into Lectra Modaris" [ANECDOTAL]](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/360038327573-Opening-a-CLO-made-AAMA-DXF-file-into-Lectra-Modaris)
- **CLO import:** a Scale field or "Auto Scale" detects the source units. — [CLO support, Import DXF [OFFICIAL]](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115012380628-Import-DXF)
- **CLO→factory issues:** users report "major issues" when factories import their CLO patterns. — [CLO forum [ANECDOTAL]](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/360038169794-PATTERN-IMPORTING-All-factories-I-have-worked-with-have-had-major-issues-when-importing-my-patterns-HELP-PLEASE-)
- **Modaris side:** tutorials exist for exporting AAMA/ASTM DXF from Modaris V8+ and V7/V6, so school-to-home is supported. — [YouTube, Exportar AAMA/ASTM desde Modaris V8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aeQ3OxyE3vc); [YouTube, Modaris V7/V6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k0eGtuC36JQ)
- **Browzwear VStitcher:** exports AAMA DXF (base size plus a reference grade-rule file). VStitcher 7.8 / Lotta 3.8 improved DXF/AAMA/ASTM import, including grading. — [Browzwear help, Importing/Exporting [OFFICIAL]](https://support.browzwear.com/VStitcher/Basic/import-file-vs.htm); [Browzwear blog, VStitcher 7.8/Lotta 3.8 [OFFICIAL]](https://browzwear.com/blog/whats-new-in-vsticher-78-lotta-38-2)
- **Style3D Studio:** has a dedicated DXF Import/Export help page. — [Style3D Studio help, DXF Import/Export [OFFICIAL]](https://help.style3d.com/studio/en/c2c8/6cc0/60d0/a589)
- **TUKAcad:** imports/exports AAMA DXF and ASTM DXF, and handles Gerber, Lectra, Optitex and Investronica data via TUKAweb's data conversion service. — [TUKAweb data conversion [OFFICIAL]](https://tukaweb.com/services/data_conversion_info)
- **Audaces Moldes:** imports AAMA-292 / ASTM D6673 DXF (from Gerber, Lectra, Optitex), **native Lectra .vet/.iba/.mdl**, and Gerber .zip. Exports DXF. — [Audaces Moldes manual v10 (third-party mirror of official manual)](https://pdfcoffee.com/manual-digital-audaces-moldes-vs10pdf-pdf-free.html)
- **Grafis:** exports AAMA/DXF and ASTM/DXF (base size in .DXF plus grade rules in .RUL). Imports AAMA DXF+RUL or all-sizes DXF. — [Grafis help, export AAMA/ASTM [OFFICIAL]](https://help.grafis.de/en-gb/Content/ExportIImport/Schrittfolge_fuer_den_Export.htm); [Grafis help, import grade-rule patterns [OFFICIAL]](https://help.grafis.de/en-gb/Content/ExportIImport/Import_von_Sprungwertschnitten.htm)

### Inferences
- **For two-way exchange with Modaris, the safest home tools are:**
  - Audaces (unusually, it reads native Lectra .mdl/.vet/.iba).
  - TUKAcad (strong conversion focus).
  - Grafis and CLO (well-documented AAMA + RUL export).
  - Browzwear and Style3D Studio (AAMA import/export).
- Round-tripping always loses Modaris-specific data: piece attributes, variant/size tables, notch types, text labels.
- Recommended practice:
  - Export in cm with grading as AAMA + RUL.
  - Re-check scale on the first import.
  - Keep the Modaris file as the "master" for anything to be graded or put into markers.
- **Modaris→CLO** (school to home) is usually smoother than **CLO→Modaris**, based on the number of forum complaints about the second direction.
